The 30-second quote

Start here

  1. Enter Client Name and confirm the Pay Frequency and Total Employees.
  2. Check the modules you want on the right (Payroll is on by default).
  3. Scroll down to the quote — it updates live.
  4. Click Print Quote at the top right to save as PDF or send to printer.
Tip
Defaults are already set for the most common quote: bi-weekly, 15 employees, Payroll module on, Client Facing view. For a standard payroll-only quote, you can just change the client name + employee count and you're done in under 30 seconds.

Client Quote vs Sales Summary

Two output modes — pick the one that fits the moment

Find the Output View selector in the left sidebar (near the middle). It swaps what shows below the module cards.

Client Quote

  • Client-friendly pricing sheet
  • Per-payroll fees prominent
  • Optional "Services Included" page
  • What you send to the customer

Sales Summary

  • Internal revenue breakdown
  • Per-month + annual + Year 1 revenue
  • Downloadable CSV for Salesforce
  • Not for client distribution
Note
Inside Client Quote view there's a second toggle — Client Facing. Turn it off to reveal the "Annual Est." column for internal review. When it's on (the default), the client sees per-payroll only.

Saving & loading quotes

Top of the Quote Settings panel

  • Save Current — name a quote and it's stored in your browser. Save the same name twice to overwrite.
  • Load — click Load next to any saved quote. Every field restores.
  • Delete — the red Delete link removes a saved quote.
Heads up
Saved quotes live only in your browser (localStorage). If you switch computers or clear browser data, they're gone. There's no shared server. Consider exporting important quotes as PDFs (Print Quote) for backup.

Hidden features (worth knowing)

Things you have to reveal

  • Ancillary Services — a toggle in Quote Settings reveals the checklist: Performance Management, ATS, COBRA, 360° 401(k), LMS, Onboarding, Expense Tracking. Also has "usage-based" services that show as informational rates on the quote (E-Verify, Job Postings, WOTC, etc.).
  • Additional Services cards on the right column — State Tax ID, PYTD Loading, and Benefit EDI are one-time / specialty fees. They live below the standard modules.
  • Sales Rep Contact Info — toggle it on in Quote Settings and fill in your name/email/phone. It'll show on the client quote's "Ready to get started?" box in gold.
  • Additional Tax Jurisdictions — inside the Payroll Processing card once selected. First state is free; each additional is $10/payroll.
  • Do Not Apply Discount — a checkbox inside the 360° 401(k) card. Excludes that module from the recurring discount %. Discounted modules show a green * on the quote.

Overrides & when to use them

Advanced knobs in Quote Settings

Payroll Base Rate override (inside the Payroll module card):

  • Default base rate is $48 bi-weekly, adjusted for other frequencies.
  • Override it for payroll-only clients where you want to charge more upfront.
  • The override is a fixed per-payroll amount — no frequency adjustment applied.

Approximate W-2s:

  • Blank = uses the employee count for year-end fees and 360° 401(k) billing.
  • Set a number for high-turnover clients where W-2 count > active headcount.

Approximate 1099s:

  • Blank = 0.
  • Set a count for clients paying contractors — the 1099s add to per-payroll fees on Payroll, TLM, HCM, and Full Service, and combine with W-2s for year-end billing.

W-2/1099 Rate:

  • Default is $6.95/form. Lower it to discount the year-end rate.

Annual W-2/1099 Forms (form count override):

  • Blank = uses your W-2 + 1099 counts above.
  • Set a number when the client's annual form count is far above per-payroll headcount (heavy turnover, restaurants, seasonal). Applies to both W-2 Processing and ACA 1094-C/1095-C fees.

Ancillary Rate Overrides:

  • 360° 401(k) and Onboarding — expand the card to override the per-employee rate and minimum. Handy for competitive scenarios.
Tip
Every override has a Reset link that returns to the default. Nothing is destructive — try things and reset if you don't like the result.

S-Corp owner-only quotes

The specialty mode

Scroll to the bottom of Quote Settings and toggle S-Corp / Owner-Only Payroll on. This dramatically simplifies the tool for these edge quotes:

  • All other modules hide — S-Corp only supports payroll.
  • Flat billing: $48/pay period bi-weekly, $250/quarter for monthly or quarterly frequencies, or $1,000/year annually.
  • Implementation defaults to $750 (adjustable).
  • Employee count defaults to 1 (the owner).
  • The Services Included page shows the S-Corp bundle by default.

Toggle it off to return to the standard multi-module quote.

Printing & exporting

Getting the quote off the screen

  • Print Quote (top right) — opens your browser's print dialog. Choose "Save as PDF" to send to the client.
  • The printed output flows to multiple pages: Page 1 = pricing quote & T&C, Page 2 = Additional Services & Rates, Page 3 = Services Included (per-module bullet lists). For S-Corp quotes, everything fits on one page.
Tip
Remove the browser URL from the printed page: in the print dialog, expand More settings and uncheck Headers and footers. This hides the URL/date/page numbers that browsers add by default. Chrome and Edge remember this preference after you set it once.
Note
When Client Facing is off, the pricing table includes an Annual Est. column for your internal review. Toggle it on before printing for the client.

Troubleshooting FAQ

Quick answers

The minimum fee is applied but the client's per-employee math doesn't add up

The module has a minimum floor. If (rate × employees) is below the minimum, the minimum is charged instead. Look for the ★ Minimum Applied badge on the module card. The rate line on the quote also shows (Min $X) only when this is happening.

My discount % isn't reducing the total by what I expected

Discounts apply only to recurring per-payroll fees, not to one-time setup, year-end fees, or opted-out modules. Modules that ARE being discounted have a green * next to their per-payroll amount. If 360° 401(k) is selected, check whether the "Do Not Apply Discount" box is checked inside its card.

I saved a quote but can't find it after switching computers

Saved quotes are per-browser only. There's no shared server. Best practice: Print Quote and save the PDF wherever you keep client work. For frequently reused quote shapes, save them locally in each browser you use.

The Sales Summary shows a Year 1 Revenue number — what's included?

Year 1 Revenue = annual recurring (after discount) + year-end fees + all one-time setup. It's the total forecasted revenue for the first year of the account.

How do 1099 contractors factor into the price?

Setting the "Approximate 1099s" field:

  • Adds to the per-payroll fee for Payroll, TLM, HCM, and Full Service (same $/emp rate applies)
  • Combines with W-2s at year-end for W-2/1099 processing
  • Does not affect ACA (only counts W-2 employees) or 360° 401(k) (only counts W-2 employees)
The client wants a bigger discount than the discount % allows for one module

Use the rate override on that module instead. For payroll, use the Payroll Base Rate override (inside the Payroll card). For 360° 401(k) or Onboarding, expand the ancillary and use the Rate override. For year-end W-2/1099, use the W-2/1099 Rate field in Quote Settings.

Where does the Additional Services section come from on the quote?

Anything in "Additional Services" is an ancillary usage-based fee (like E-Verify $3.50/new hire) or a one-time specialty fee (State Tax ID, PYTD, Benefit EDI implementation). They appear on the client quote below the main pricing table.

The quote looks weird after I toggle S-Corp mode

S-Corp mode changes several defaults at once (employee count → 1, other modules hide, ancillary services hide). Toggle S-Corp off to return to the standard multi-module view. Any values you had before are not automatically restored — reload a saved quote if you had one, or reconfigure.

How do I show my own contact info on the quote?

In Quote Settings, toggle on Sales Rep Contact, then fill in your name / phone / email. It'll appear in gold on the "Ready to get started?" box at the bottom of the client-facing quote.
