How to update the passcode

  1. Type the new passcode above and copy the hash.
  2. Open src/constants/access.js in the GitHub repo and click the pencil icon to edit.
  3. Add the hash to the ACCESS_CODE_HASHES list, with a comment noting which passcode it is and the date. To retire the old passcode, delete its line; to let both work for a while, keep both.
  4. Commit to the main branch. The site rebuilds itself in about two minutes.
Anyone already signed in stays signed in as long as their passcode is still in the list. Delete a hash and everyone who used that passcode is asked for a new one on their next visit.
