A quote in three steps.

A quick reference for building, adjusting and saving your proposal.

01 · Configure

Enter the company, active employee count and pay frequency. Standard rates adjust automatically. Open Different W-2 or contractor counts? when those counts differ.

Use Pricing preferences for the recurring discount, annual form adjustments or the owner-only S-Corp scenario. Add your contact information under Make it personal; blank fields stay off the quote.

02 · Services

Start with core modules, then browse recurring add-ons, specialty services or usage-based rates. Search works within the selected category. Selected only gives you a shorter checklist.

  • Setup fees: select a service, then change its setup amount or turn setup off to waive it.
  • Rate overrides: open Rate & discount adjustments inside the service. Payroll's base override stays fixed when frequency changes. 401(k) and onboarding overrides are biweekly baseline rates and scale with frequency.
  • Discount exclusions: check Do not apply recurring discount on a service you want to keep at full price.
  • Different counts: Expense Tracking has its own user field. Payroll has an additional tax-jurisdiction field. Blank optional counts use the stated default.
  • Usage rates: these appear for reference and do not increase the estimate.

03 · Review & export

Client facing includes the pricing quote, separate annual recap and ancillary rate sheet, and selected included services. Internal sales puts annual estimates alongside each module for internal review.

Choose Print / Save PDF. In your browser's print dialog, select Save as PDF and turn off Headers and footers to remove the browser URL and date.

Save now. Pick up later.

Open Saved quotes in the header, name your quote and save it. Use Load to return to a draft. Reusing a name asks you to confirm replacement.

Drafts live in this browser on this computer. Save before closing the tab. Export a PDF for a permanent copy; clearing browser data removes drafts. Preview drafts are separate from your original Quote Builder drafts.

If a number looks unexpected

  • Check the frequency, service count, minimum and discount exclusion shown on the card.
  • Reset custom rates or clear optional form counts to return to defaults.
  • Recurring annual estimates exclude setup, annual processing and usage rates; the internal inline annual column includes annual processing.
  • S-Corp mode can always be turned off under Configure → Pricing preferences → Special payroll scenario.
